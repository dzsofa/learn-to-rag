### Based on https://www.trychroma.com/research/evaluating-chunking ###

| Strategy (as in report) | Core idea | How boundaries are chosen | Chunk size behavior | Overlap | Main strengths | Main weaknesses / risks | Best when | **Observed tendency in the report** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **TokenTextSplitter** (fixed-size) | Split by token count | Every `chunk_size` tokens (optionally sliding by overlap) | Very consistent | Optional (common) | Fast, simple, predictable | Cuts mid-sentence/section; overlap adds redundancy | Simple baseline | With **large overlap (e.g., 800/400)**: *poor efficiency* (low precision/IoU). With **weaker embedder (MiniLM)**: overlap can **help recall** (reduces boundary loss). |
| **RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter** (structure-aware fixed-size) | Target size but prefer natural separators | Recursively split by separators (paragraph→line→sentence→space) | Mostly consistent, some variance | Optional | Coherent chunks; often better than pure fixed-size | Separator tuning required | General docs/prose | Typically a **strong baseline**; **200–400 tokens with little/no overlap** performed well overall (good recall–efficiency tradeoff). |
| **KamradtSemanticChunker** (semantic discontinuity) | Split at meaning shifts | Embed units; cut when adjacent similarity drops below threshold/percentile | Variable; can produce large chunks | Usually none | Topic-aligned boundaries | Threshold instability; uneven sizes | Topic-shifty corpora | **Slightly below average** in their results as implemented; chunk-size variability can hurt consistency. |
| **KamradtModifiedChunker** (size-controlled semantic) | Semantic splitting + max size constraint | Binary-search/adjust threshold to keep chunks under max | Variable but **bounded max** | Usually none | More stable than default semantic chunker | More compute/complexity | Need semantic boundaries + size bounds | **Improves** over default Kamradt (better recall/efficiency balance) by preventing overly large chunks. |
| **ClusterSemanticChunker** (clustering-based semantic) | Group neighboring units by semantic similarity | Embed units; cluster/group into coherent chunks under max size (order-aware) | Variable but **bounded max** | Usually none | Dense, relevant chunks; strong efficiency | More compute; clustering choices matter | Token-efficiency sensitive RAG | **Best efficiency** at smaller sizes (best precision/IoU around max 200). At max 400, recall is **near the top** (2nd best in their table). |
| **LLMChunker / LLMSemanticChunker** (LLM-driven) | LLM decides chunk boundaries | Prompt LLM for coherent chunks under constraints | Configurable; depends on prompt | Typically none | Very coherent/human-like chunking | Cost/latency; variability | Highest-quality chunking | **Best recall** in their table (top performer), but efficiency metrics are not the best; tradeoff is cost/latency. |
